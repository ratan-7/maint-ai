# Motor Maintenance Manual

## Excessive Vibration

### Possible Causes

- Bearing wear
- Shaft misalignment
- Rotor imbalance
- Loose mounting
- Coupling misalignment

### Inspection Steps

1. Check motor mounting bolts.
2. Inspect motor and pump alignment.
3. Check bearings for unusual noise or play.
4. Inspect coupling condition.
5. Check for abnormal rotor vibration.

### Evidence

Motor vibration may be associated with bearing wear, imbalance, looseness, or alignment problems. The suspected cause must be confirmed through inspection.

---

## Motor Overheating

### Possible Causes

- Excessive load
- Poor ventilation
- Bearing friction
- Electrical imbalance
- Insufficient cooling
- Incorrect operating conditions

### Inspection Steps

1. Check motor load.
2. Inspect ventilation openings.
3. Check cooling fan operation.
4. Inspect bearings.
5. Verify electrical supply conditions.
6. Check for abnormal operating conditions.

### Evidence

Motor overheating can result from excessive load, inadequate cooling, mechanical friction, or electrical problems.

---

## Motor Noise

### Possible Causes

- Bearing damage
- Loose components
- Misalignment
- Electrical problems
- Mechanical interference

### Inspection Steps

1. Identify the source of the noise.
2. Inspect bearings.
3. Check mounting and fasteners.
4. Inspect coupling alignment.
5. Verify that rotating components are not contacting surrounding parts.

### Evidence

Unusual motor noise requires inspection to distinguish mechanical and electrical causes.
